# SPEC - EDGE Legal v0

## Scope
EDGE Legal governs **legal-domain** requests at the decision layer only. It never produces legal advice, interpretations, or drafted documents.

## Non-Goals
- No legal advice or opinions.
- No jurisdiction-specific guidance.
- No drafting or review of legal documents.

## Refusal vs Escalation Philosophy
Legal is **default-conservative** due to high liability risk.
- **REFUSE** for advice, drafting, interpretation, or jurisdiction-specific queries.
- **ESCALATE** for disputes, litigation, criminal exposure, or regulatory enforcement.
- **ASK_CLARIFY** for ambiguous process references or third-party situations.
- **PROCEED** only for high-level educational questions about legal concepts.

## Determinism
All decisions are schema-driven and deterministic. Same input + same version → same decision.
