EDGE — Authorization Before AI Action

EDGE is a deterministic inference-time authorization layer for AI systems. It executes before model generation and returns exactly one of four decision states: PROCEED, ASK_CLARIFY, REFUSE, or ESCALATE.

Deterministic decisions. Replayable outcomes. Fail-closed enforcement.

The Missing Primitive in the AI Stack

AI systems today combine three layers:

  • Model reasoning
  • Retrieval and context
  • Tool execution

What is absent is a deterministic authorization boundary between intent and execution.

Without this boundary, control lives inside a probabilistic system.

EDGE introduces that boundary.

It does not improve reasoning. It does not filter outputs. It does not modify models.

It authorizes whether action may occur at all.

In distributed systems, reliability required TCP/IP. In web security, reliability required TLS. In enterprise networks, reliability required firewalls.

AI systems require a deterministic authorization layer.

EDGE exists to provide that layer.

For Teams Who Cannot Afford AI Failure

EDGE is built for systems where unpredictability is unacceptable.

  • Medical decision support
  • Financial authorization and underwriting
  • Legal reasoning and compliance automation
  • Insurance claims and risk engines
  • Enterprise copilots operating in regulated workflows

If model output influences money, medicine, contracts, or regulatory exposure, authorization must precede generation.
